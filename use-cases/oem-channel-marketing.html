Turn your partner network into a measurable marketing channel.

Help distributors, dealers, resellers, and other channel partners generate demand, while giving your team visibility into the contribution behind the campaign.

◆The problem

Creating the campaign was never the hard part.

Manufacturers and OEMs usually rely on a network of partners to reach the market. The challenge is understanding what happens after the campaign reaches that network.

Which partners are actively promoting? Which are generating leads? Which locations are contributing? Which partners are worth investing in further?
⚙How Gamiflow helps

One chain, from brand to CRM.

Brand

Creates and manages the campaign.

→

Partner

Receives a measurable way to distribute it.

→

Audience

Participates in the campaign.

→

Gamiflow

Connects the activity back to its source.

→

CRM

Receives the resulting data for follow-up.

Gamiflow doesn't replace your CRM or sales process. It creates a clearer connection between campaign distribution and the leads generated from that distribution.
