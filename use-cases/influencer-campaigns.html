🎤Influencer Campaigns

Turn influencer reach into emails, coupon redemptions, and a real ROAS number.

The most common version of this use case is an e-commerce brand running a giveaway through a group of influencers, capturing emails at the top, and tracking every one of those emails through to a coupon-code purchase. Here's exactly how that's built.

📍Where this fits

Influencer campaigns touch three stages, not one.

Most teams think of influencer marketing as pure awareness. In practice, one campaign gives you a lead-capture moment, a purchase-tracking moment, and a retention moment, if it's built to capture data at each one.

🧪Real-world implementation

How a DTC skincare brand actually runs this.

🧴 Bloom & Co., DTC skincare, Shopify store

Bloom & Co. wants to grow its email list ahead of a seasonal sale and prove which influencers are worth rebooking. They partner with 12 micro-influencers (8,000 to 60,000 followers each).

The setup

  1. Each influencer gets their own tracked entry link (gamiflow.com/bloom/via/mia) and their own coupon code (MIA20), generated from the same campaign.
  2. The campaign is a gamified giveaway: entering with an email is the base action, and following Bloom's Instagram or sharing the post earns bonus entries.
  3. Every entrant, winner or not, receives their influencer's 20% coupon code by email immediately, so the campaign converts even for people who don't win anything.
  4. Influencers post the link in their Stories and bio for 7 to 10 days. Gamiflow's detection layer filters bot and duplicate entries before anyone touches the list.
  5. Coupon codes are unique per influencer, so when someone checks out on Shopify, the code itself tells you exactly which creator gets the credit.

The result Bloom's team actually looks at

Not "how many entries." A per-influencer breakdown of emails captured, coupon redemptions, revenue generated, and cost, so the next rebooking decision is based on ROAS, not follower count.

🔌How it connects to your existing stack

It doesn't replace Shopify or Klaviyo. It feeds them.

01Entry captured on the campaign page, with the influencer source attached automatically from the tracked link.
02New email pushed to Klaviyo or Mailchimp as a segmented list ("Bloom Giveaway, via Mia"), so follow-up emails can be written per influencer if needed.
03Coupon code lives in Shopify as a normal discount code. No custom checkout work required.
04A Shopify order webhook fires back to Gamiflow whenever that code is used, matching the redemption to the original entry and influencer.
05Revenue and redemption counts roll up into a per-influencer scorecard your team checks before the next booking, not a spreadsheet someone rebuilds by hand.
