🎤Influencer Campaigns

Turn influencer reach into measurable campaign results.

Give every influencer a measurable way to promote your campaign, and see which creators are generating participation and leads.

Start Building a Campaign
◆The problem

Reach and impressions don't tell you who took action.

They tell you how many people may have seen a campaign. They don't tell you who actually did something about it. Gamiflow connects campaign activity back to the influencer who generated it.

⚙How it works

From creator handoff to a real leaderboard.

1

Create the campaign

Build a giveaway or lead-generation experience.

2

Add influencers

Give each creator a measurable way to distribute the campaign.

3

Let them promote

Creators share the campaign with their own audiences.

4

Capture the leads

Participants complete the campaign experience.

5

Attribute the activity

See which influencer generated the resulting participation.

6

Compare performance

Identify the creators contributing the most.

🏆Gamified influencer campaigns

Turn promotion into competition.

Create a leaderboard that shows campaign performance across participating creators. It motivates creators, recognizes top performers, and encourages continued promotion. The campaign becomes more than a sponsored post, it becomes a measurable distribution channel.
