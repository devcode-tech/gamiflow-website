◆The attribution problem

Your campaign can have many sources. Your attribution should not disappear.

A campaign may be shared by multiple people and organizations. Once the traffic starts arriving, it can become difficult to answer the questions that actually matter.

Which influencer generated this lead? Which distributor is actively promoting? Which store generated the most participation? Which partner is contributing the most demand? Which channel deserves more investment?

Channel gives your campaign a way to connect activity back to its source, so those questions have real answers instead of guesses.

⤳Distribution

Give every source a measurable way to promote your campaign.

Distribution lets a campaign be shared through multiple sources while preserving attribution. The campaign doesn't need to be rebuilt for every source, the source just needs a measurable way to distribute it.
