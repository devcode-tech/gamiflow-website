Billing, conversions, and plan changes.

A conversion is one completed campaign action tied to a lead: an entry submitted, a form completed, or a gamified action finished. Browsing a campaign without completing it doesn't count.

You're billed for additional conversions at your plan's per-unit rate, shown on every plan card and in the comparison table above. There's no hard cutoff, your campaigns keep running.

Yearly plans are billed annually at a lower effective monthly rate, and come with a larger monthly conversion allowance on top of the discount.
