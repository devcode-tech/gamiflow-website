⤳Distribution

Distribute one campaign through your entire network.

Give influencers, partners, stores, distributors, and other sources a measurable way to promote your campaign.

Start Building a Campaign
◆Everywhere, without losing the source

Your campaign can be everywhere without losing its source.

A single campaign can be distributed across multiple sources, and each source can be connected to the activity it generates. That means moving beyond "the campaign generated 5,000 leads" and starting to ask "which sources generated those leads?"

⚙Distribution workflow

From one campaign to a full network.

1

Create campaign

Build the giveaway, lead-generation campaign, or interactive experience.

2

Add sources

Bring in the influencers, partners, stores, or distributors who'll share it.

3

Distribute

Each source gets its own measurable way to promote the campaign.

4

Capture activity

Participants complete the campaign experience.

5

Attribute results

See which source produced each lead.

6

Compare performance

Rank sources and decide where to invest next.
