◆The Conversion problem

A giveaway is the mechanism. The data is the asset.

A campaign can generate thousands of entries. But the real value comes from what happens next.

Capture useful customer information? Collect clear consent? Encourage meaningful actions? Keep invalid participation out of your data? Move the lead into your existing workflows?

Gamiflow is designed to make the campaign itself a useful part of your customer acquisition process, not just a prize draw that happens once and disappears.

🎮Gamified actions

Give participants more ways to engage.

Additional entries let you reward the actions that matter to your campaign. You decide which actions to include and how much they contribute to a participant's chances.

✅Consent

Collect data with clear participant consent.

When you collect first-party data, the participant should understand what they're agreeing to. Gamiflow supports consent collection as part of the campaign experience, so brands build audiences with a clearer relationship between participation and data collection.

🛡️Data quality

Keep invalid and suspicious participation from polluting your campaign.

Campaigns can attract automated activity and low-quality submissions. The goal isn't claiming that every bad actor gets eliminated. It's giving your campaign a better foundation for identifying and reducing low-quality participation.
