◆Why attribution matters

Most campaigns tell you what happened. Gamiflow helps you understand where it came from.

A campaign may generate thousands of entries or leads. But the important question is often left unanswered: who actually generated them?

An influencer? A distributor? A retail store? A dealer? An event? A marketing partner?

Gamiflow connects campaign activity back to the source that generated it. That gives your team a clearer view of not just campaign performance, but the channels behind that performance.

🛡️Data quality & protection

Better campaign data starts with better participation.

Campaigns can attract automated activity, invalid addresses, and suspicious participation. Gamiflow includes multiple layers designed to help protect the quality of your campaign data.

🤖

Bot & automated activity protection

A dedicated protection layer helps reduce automated and abusive participation.

📧

Email verification

Enabled by default, to help identify invalid or suspicious entries before they count.

🚩

Suspicious activity flagging

Potentially suspicious participation gets flagged for review instead of being treated as normal activity.
