"Where did this lead come from?"

The source may change, an influencer, a distributor, a dealer, a store, an affiliate. The underlying question stays exactly the same, and Gamiflow's attribution model is built to answer it regardless of what kind of source is involved.

📊What it looks like

A number becomes a lot more useful once you can see its parts.

A campaign generating 10,000 leads is a good headline. Knowing that Influencer A generated 324 of them, Distributor B generated 218, and Store C generated 97, is what actually helps your team make decisions.
