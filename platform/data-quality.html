🛡️Data Quality & Protection

Protect the quality of your campaign data.

Campaigns can attract automated participation, invalid email addresses, and suspicious activity. Gamiflow includes multiple layers designed to help reduce low-quality campaign activity.

Start Building a Campaign
🤖

Bot protection

A dedicated protection layer helps reduce automated and abusive participation before it reaches your results.

📧

Email verification

Enabled by default, to help identify invalid or suspicious entries as they come in.

🚩

Suspicious activity

Potentially suspicious participation gets flagged for review instead of counted as normal.

◆An honest note

We won't tell you every bad actor gets caught.

No system eliminates every piece of automated or suspicious activity. What these layers do is give your campaign a stronger foundation, so your team spends less time manually sifting through entries and more time acting on the ones that matter.

Reduce noise. Protect campaign quality. Make your data more useful.
