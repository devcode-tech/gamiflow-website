🎮Gamification

Make participation more engaging.

Use additional entries and campaign actions to reward the behavior that matters to your campaign.

Start Building a Campaign
◆Core concept

Action. Additional entry. More chances.

The actions are defined by the campaign. The reward structure is controlled by the campaign owner. Nothing is locked to a fixed template.

👥 Join a community→ +1 📬 Subscribe to a channel→ +1 ⭐ Follow a brand→ +1
🔁 Share a campaign→ +1 📝 Complete a form→ +1 ➕ Another defined action→ +1
🎯Why it matters

More meaningful actions. More engagement. Better campaign data.

A referral link earns a share of that person's audience. A newsletter subscription earns a direct line of communication. A completed form earns the exact data your team needs. Gamification turns a one-time entry into a series of small, valuable actions, each one worth rewarding on its own terms.

🎁Where it's used

Gamification shows up across both engines.

🎁

In Conversion

Reward the actions that grow your audience: referrals, subscriptions, shares, community joins.

📡

In Channel

Turn promotion into competition with a leaderboard that ranks sources by the participation they generate.
